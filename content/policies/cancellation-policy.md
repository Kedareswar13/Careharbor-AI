# Appointment booking, rescheduling and cancellation policy

## Booking
- Appointments can be booked up to **14 days ahead** through CareHarbor AI chat, by phone or at the front desk.
- Existing patients must verify their identity (phone number, date of birth and a one-time code) before booking online.
- One patient can hold a maximum of **3 upcoming appointments** at a time.

## Rescheduling
- You can reschedule free of charge up to **2 hours** before the appointment time.
- An appointment can be rescheduled a maximum of 2 times; after that please call the front desk.

## Cancellation
- Cancel at least **2 hours** before the appointment so the slot can be given to another patient.
- There is no cancellation charge, because fees are paid at the visit.
- Prepaid teleconsultations cancelled at least 2 hours before are refunded in full within 5–7 working days.

## No-shows
If you miss 3 appointments in 6 months without cancelling, online booking may be paused and you will need to book at the front desk.

## Doctor unavailability
If a doctor is unavailable, we will contact you by SMS and offer the next available slot or another doctor in the same department.
