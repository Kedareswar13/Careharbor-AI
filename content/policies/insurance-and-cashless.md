# Insurance and cashless treatment

## Insurers we work with
We accept the following insurers and TPAs for **OPD cashless** benefits (only if your policy includes OPD cover):
- Star Health and Allied Insurance
- HDFC ERGO Health
- ICICI Lombard
- Niva Bupa
- Care Health Insurance
- Medi Assist (TPA)

We do **not** currently accept CGHS or ECHS for cashless billing. You can pay and claim reimbursement.

## How cashless works
1. Show your insurance health card and a government photo ID at registration.
2. The front desk checks your OPD eligibility with the insurer (usually 10–20 minutes).
3. If approved, you pay only the co-payment or any non-covered items.

## Reimbursement
If your policy does not include cashless OPD, pay at the clinic and ask for an itemised bill, the prescription and reports. Most insurers require claims within 30 days of the visit.

## Notes
- Consumables, registration fees and non-prescribed items may not be covered.
- For questions about your specific policy coverage, please contact your insurer.
