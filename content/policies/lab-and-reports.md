# Laboratory tests and reports

## Sample collection
- Monday to Saturday, **7:30 AM to 11:30 AM** at the ground-floor lab.
- **Fasting tests** (fasting blood sugar, lipid profile): no food for 10–12 hours before; plain water is allowed. Please come before 10:00 AM.
- **Home sample collection** is available within 5 km for ₹200 extra. Book a day in advance at the front desk.

## Report timings
| Test type | Report ready |
|---|---|
| Routine blood tests (CBC, sugar, thyroid, kidney, liver) | Same day by 6:00 PM |
| Urine and stool routine | Same day by 4:00 PM |
| Cultures | 48–72 hours |
| X-ray | Within 2 hours |
| Specialised tests sent to partner labs | 3–5 working days |

## Collecting reports
Reports are sent by SMS link and can be collected at the front desk with the bill number. Reports are released only to the patient or an authorised family member.

## Understanding your results
Please discuss results with your doctor. CareHarbor AI cannot interpret lab results. Follow-up review within 7 days with the same doctor is free.
