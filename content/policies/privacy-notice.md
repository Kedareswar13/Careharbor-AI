# Privacy notice

## What we collect
Name, phone number, date of birth, appointment details, prescriptions and chat messages needed to provide care and run the clinic.

## How CareHarbor AI protects your information
- CareHarbor AI shows appointment and prescription details **only after you verify your identity** with your phone number, date of birth and a one-time code.
- Phone numbers and dates of birth are **masked in system logs**.
- Every access to records and every action (booking, cancellation, handoff) is recorded in an audit log.
- CareHarbor AI cannot see other patients' records.

## How we use your information
Only to provide care, manage appointments, send reminders and meet legal requirements. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Your rights
Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent. Contact the front desk or privacy@sunrise-clinic.example.

## AI assistant notice
CareHarbor AI is an automated assistant. It does not diagnose illness or prescribe medicines, and it is not a substitute for a doctor. You can ask to speak to a person at any time.

This is a demonstration project. All data is synthetic.
