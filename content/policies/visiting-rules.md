# Visiting rules and what to bring

## Companions
- Each patient may bring **one companion** into the consultation room. Children and patients who need support may bring two.
- Children under 12 should not come as visitors unless they are the patient.
- Please wear a mask if you have a cough, cold or fever.

## What to bring
- Government photo ID and your appointment confirmation (SMS or CareHarbor AI chat).
- All current medicines or their strips, and previous prescriptions.
- Previous reports, X-rays or scans.
- Insurance card, if you want to use cashless OPD.

## Arrival
Please arrive **15 minutes before** your appointment time for registration and vitals. If you are more than 15 minutes late, you may be seen after the next available patient.

## Accessibility and parking
- Step-free entrance and a lift to all floors. Wheelchairs are available at reception.
- Limited free parking in the basement; two-wheeler parking at the side gate.

## Conduct
Please treat staff with respect. Photography and video recording inside consultation rooms is not allowed.
