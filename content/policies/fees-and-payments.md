# Fees and payments

## Consultation fees
| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| General Medicine consultation | ₹500 |
| Paediatrics consultation | ₹600 |
| Specialist consultation (Dermatology, ENT, Orthopaedics, Gynaecology) | ₹800 |
| Cardiology consultation | ₹1,000 |
| Physiotherapy session (45 minutes) | ₹700 |
| Teleconsultation (Sunday) | ₹400 |
| One-time registration (new patients) | ₹100 |

## Follow-up visits
A follow-up with the **same doctor within 7 days** of the first consultation, for the same problem, is **free**. Between 8 and 14 days it is charged at 50%.

## Payment methods
UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash are accepted. Cheques are not accepted.

## Senior citizens
Patients aged 65 and above receive a 10% discount on consultation fees (show age proof).

All prices are fictional and for demonstration only.
