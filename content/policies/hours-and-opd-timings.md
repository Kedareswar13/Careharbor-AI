# Hours and OPD timings

Sunrise Family Clinic (synthetic demo clinic), 14 Lake View Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038.

## OPD (outpatient) timings
- **Monday to Saturday:** 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- **Sunday:** closed for OPD. Teleconsultation with the duty doctor from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
- **National holidays:** closed. Holiday dates are posted on the notice board and the website one week in advance.
- Registration desk opens 15 minutes before OPD and closes 30 minutes before OPD ends.

## Other services
- **Pharmacy:** Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM; Sunday 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- **Laboratory sample collection:** Monday to Saturday 7:30 AM to 11:30 AM (fasting samples before 10:00 AM).
- **Vaccination clinic:** Wednesday and Saturday, 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

## Walk-ins
Walk-in patients are seen between booked appointments, so waiting times can be longer. Booking an appointment through CareHarbor AI or the front desk is recommended.

## Emergencies
The clinic is not an emergency hospital. In an emergency call **112** or **108**, or go to the nearest emergency department.
