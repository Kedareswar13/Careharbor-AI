## Which department should I choose?
- Not sure? Start with **General Medicine**; Dr. Rao will refer you if needed.
- Children under 14 years: **Paediatrics**.
- Specialist appointments (Cardiology, Orthopaedics) do not need a referral, but please bring previous reports.
