# Seasonal allergy (hay fever)

## About
Allergic rhinitis causes sneezing, a runny or blocked nose and itchy, watery eyes. Common triggers are pollen, dust mites, pet dander, mould and smoke. It is not contagious.

## Self-care
- Notice your triggers and reduce contact: keep windows closed on high-pollen or dusty days.
- Wash bed sheets weekly in hot water and use dust-mite covers if dust is a trigger.
- Shower and change clothes after being outdoors.
- Rinse your nose with saline.
- Wear a mask while sweeping, dusting or travelling on dusty roads.
- Ask a doctor or pharmacist before starting any allergy medicine, especially if you drive or operate machinery.

## See a doctor if
- Symptoms disturb your sleep, work or studies.
- You also get wheezing, chest tightness or a night-time cough (possible asthma).
- You have facial pain, thick nasal discharge or fever.
- Over-the-counter advice has not helped after 2 weeks.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Difficulty breathing or wheezing that does not settle.
- Swelling of the lips, tongue or throat.
- Feeling faint or collapsing after exposure to a trigger.
