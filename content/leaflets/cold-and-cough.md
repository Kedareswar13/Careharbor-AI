# Common cold and cough

## About
Colds are viral infections of the nose and throat. They usually get better on their own in 7–10 days, although a cough can last up to 3 weeks. Antibiotics do not help a cold.

## Self-care
- Rest, and drink warm fluids such as water, soups, ginger or tulsi tea.
- Steam inhalation over a bowl of hot water (keep a safe distance) can ease a blocked nose. Never use it with children close to hot water.
- Saline nasal drops or rinses can help clear the nose.
- Honey in warm water can soothe a cough in adults and children over 1 year. Never give honey to babies under 1 year.
- Cover coughs and sneezes, wash your hands often, and wear a mask around elderly or unwell people.
- Ask a pharmacist before using any cough or cold medicine; many contain the same ingredients.

## See a doctor if
- Symptoms last more than 10 days, or improve and then get worse.
- Fever is above 102 °F or lasts more than 3 days.
- You have ear pain, facial pain, or thick green or bloody phlegm.
- A cough lasts more than 3 weeks, or you are losing weight or sweating at night.
- You have asthma, COPD, heart disease or diabetes and your symptoms are worse than usual.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Difficulty breathing, breathlessness at rest, or noisy breathing.
- Chest pain or pressure.
- Bluish lips or face.
- Coughing up blood.
- Confusion or extreme drowsiness.
