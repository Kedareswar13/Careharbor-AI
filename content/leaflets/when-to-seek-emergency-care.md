# When to seek emergency care

## About
Some symptoms need help within minutes, not hours. In India call **112** (all emergencies) or **108** (ambulance), or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for an appointment and do not drive yourself if you feel unwell.

## Self-care
While help is coming:
- Stay with the person, keep them calm and still, and loosen tight clothing.
- If they are unconscious but breathing, turn them onto their side (recovery position).
- Do not give food or drink to someone who is drowsy, confused or about to have surgery.
- Bring the person's medicines and recent prescriptions or reports to the hospital.

## See a doctor if
- You are unsure whether a symptom is serious: call the clinic and ask to speak to the duty nurse. If in doubt, treat it as an emergency.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- **Chest pain or pressure**, especially spreading to the arm, jaw or back, or with sweating or breathlessness.
- **Stroke signs (BE FAST):** loss of Balance, Eye/vision changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech slurred - Time to call.
- **Breathing difficulty**, choking, or blue lips.
- **Sudden severe headache** ("worst ever"), or headache with stiff neck and fever.
- **Fainting, fits (seizures), or sudden confusion.**
- **Severe bleeding** that will not stop, or vomiting blood.
- **Severe allergic reaction:** swelling of the face, lips or throat.
- **Thoughts of suicide or self-harm** - also Tele-MANAS 14416.
- **Pregnancy** with bleeding, severe tummy pain, fits or severe headache.
- **Babies under 3 months with any fever**, or a child who is floppy, unusually drowsy or not feeding.
- Serious injury, burns, poisoning or overdose.
