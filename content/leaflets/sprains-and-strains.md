# Sprains and strains

## About
A sprain is a stretched or torn ligament (often the ankle or wrist). A strain is a stretched muscle. Both cause pain, swelling and bruising and usually improve within 2–6 weeks.

## Self-care
For the first 48–72 hours:
- **Protect:** stop the activity and support the joint.
- **Rest:** avoid putting weight on it for the first day or two, then slowly start moving.
- **Ice:** wrap ice or a cold pack in a towel and apply for 15–20 minutes every 2–3 hours. Never put ice directly on the skin.
- **Compress:** use an elastic bandage that is snug but not tight.
- **Elevate:** keep the injured part raised above the level of the heart when you can.
- Avoid heat, alcohol and massage for the first 2–3 days.
- After a few days, gentle movement helps healing. Our physiotherapist can guide exercises.

## See a doctor if
- You cannot put any weight on the leg, or use the hand, after 24 hours.
- The pain and swelling are not improving after 5–7 days.
- The joint feels unstable, gives way or locks.
- You heard a crack at the time of injury.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- The limb looks bent or deformed, or bone is visible.
- The foot or hand is cold, pale, blue or numb.
- Injury after a fall from height or a road accident, especially with neck or back pain.
