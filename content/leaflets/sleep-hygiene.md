# Sleep hygiene

## About
Most adults need 7–9 hours of sleep. Short-term sleep trouble is common during stress, travel or illness. Good sleep habits ("sleep hygiene") help most people.

## Self-care
- Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, including weekends.
- Keep the bedroom dark, quiet and cool. Use the bed only for sleep.
- Stop screens (phone, laptop, TV) at least 30–60 minutes before bed.
- Avoid tea, coffee and cola after 3 PM, and avoid alcohol and smoking in the evening.
- Have dinner 2–3 hours before bed. Avoid heavy meals late at night.
- Get daylight and some physical activity during the day, but not intense exercise just before bed.
- If you cannot sleep after 20 minutes, get up and do something calm in dim light, then return to bed.
- Do not start sleeping tablets without a doctor's advice.

## See a doctor if
- Poor sleep lasts more than 3–4 weeks or affects your work or mood.
- You snore loudly, stop breathing during sleep, or feel very sleepy during the day.
- You have low mood, anxiety, or lose interest in things you enjoy.
- You have restless legs, pain or frequent urination at night.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life. Please call 112 now, or the Tele-MANAS helpline 14416, or go to the nearest emergency department. You do not have to face this alone.
- Falling asleep while driving or operating machinery is dangerous: stop driving and seek medical review urgently.
