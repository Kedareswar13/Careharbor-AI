# Sore throat

## About
Most sore throats are caused by viruses and get better within a week without antibiotics. Some are caused by bacteria and may need a doctor's assessment.

## Self-care
- Gargle with warm salt water (half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water) a few times a day.
- Drink warm fluids and rest your voice.
- Soft, cool or warm foods can be easier to swallow.
- Avoid smoking and smoky rooms.
- Lozenges can help adults; do not give them to small children because of choking risk.

## See a doctor if
- The sore throat lasts more than a week.
- You have a high fever, white patches on the tonsils, or swollen tender neck glands without a cough.
- You get a rash, ear pain, or the pain is much worse on one side.
- Sore throats keep coming back.
- You have a weak immune system.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Difficulty breathing or noisy breathing.
- Unable to swallow saliva, or drooling.
- Unable to open the mouth fully, or a muffled "hot potato" voice.
- Swelling of the neck or tongue.
