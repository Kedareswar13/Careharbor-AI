# Acidity and acid reflux

## About
Acidity (heartburn) is a burning feeling behind the breastbone, often after meals or when lying down. It happens when stomach acid flows back into the food pipe.

## Self-care
- Eat smaller meals and avoid lying down for 2–3 hours after eating.
- Have dinner early. Raise the head end of the bed slightly.
- Cut down on spicy, oily and fried food, tea, coffee, fizzy drinks, alcohol and smoking.
- Notice your personal triggers and avoid them.
- Lose weight if you are overweight, and avoid tight belts.
- Ask a doctor or pharmacist before using antacids regularly.

## See a doctor if
- Heartburn happens more than twice a week or lasts more than 2 weeks.
- You have difficulty or pain when swallowing, or food feels stuck.
- You are losing weight without trying, or have a poor appetite.
- You keep vomiting.
- You are over 55 and the symptoms are new.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Chest pain or pressure, especially spreading to the arm, jaw or back, or with sweating or breathlessness. Do not assume chest pain is acidity.
- Vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds.
- Black, tarry stools.
- Severe, constant stomach pain.
