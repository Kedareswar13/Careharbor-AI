# Minor cuts and burns

## About
Small cuts, scrapes and minor burns (redness or a small blister smaller than your palm) can usually be treated at home with good first aid.

## Self-care
**For cuts:**
- Wash your hands, then rinse the cut under clean running water.
- Press firmly with a clean cloth for 10 minutes to stop bleeding.
- Cover with a clean, non-stick dressing and change it daily or when wet.
- Check that your tetanus vaccination is up to date (usually every 10 years).

**For burns:**
- Cool the burn under cool running tap water for 20 minutes as soon as possible.
- Remove rings, watches or tight clothing near the burn before it swells.
- Cover loosely with cling film or a clean, non-fluffy cloth.
- Do NOT apply ice, toothpaste, butter, oil or turmeric. Do not burst blisters.

## See a doctor if
- The cut is deep, gaping, longer than 1 cm on the face, or has dirt you cannot remove.
- The wound was caused by an animal or human bite, or a rusty object.
- Signs of infection appear: spreading redness, warmth, pus, increasing pain or fever.
- A burn is larger than your palm, or is on the face, hands, feet, joints or genitals.
- You are not sure about your tetanus protection.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Bleeding that does not stop after 10 minutes of firm pressure, or spurting blood.
- Burns that look white, leathery or charred, or burns from electricity or chemicals.
- Burns with breathing difficulty, or after smoke inhalation.
- Numbness, or being unable to move the fingers or toes beyond the wound.
