# Managing blood pressure readings at home

## About
Home blood pressure (BP) readings help your doctor see how your BP behaves in daily life. A typical target for most adults is below 130/80 mmHg, but your doctor will tell you your personal target.

## How to measure correctly
- Use a validated automatic upper-arm monitor with the right cuff size.
- Avoid tea, coffee, smoking and exercise for 30 minutes before measuring.
- Empty your bladder, then sit quietly with your back supported for 5 minutes.
- Rest your arm on a table at heart level, feet flat, legs uncrossed. Do not talk.
- Take 2 readings 1 minute apart, morning and evening, for 7 days before a review.
- Write down every reading with the date and time, or bring the monitor to your visit.

## Self-care
- Reduce salt: avoid pickles, papad, chips and extra salt at the table.
- Walk briskly for 30 minutes on most days. Keep a healthy weight.
- Limit alcohol and stop smoking.
- Take BP medicines exactly as prescribed. Do not stop or change them without speaking to your doctor.

## See a doctor if
- Your readings are often above 140/90 mmHg.
- Your readings are often below 90/60 mmHg, or you feel dizzy when standing.
- You notice ankle swelling, or a new side effect after starting a medicine.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- BP of 180/120 mmHg or higher with chest pain, breathlessness, severe headache, confusion, blurred vision, or weakness on one side.
- Face drooping, arm weakness or slurred speech (possible stroke) - act FAST.
