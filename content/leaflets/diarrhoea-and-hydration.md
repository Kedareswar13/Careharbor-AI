# Diarrhoea and hydration

## About
Diarrhoea means passing loose or watery stools three or more times a day. It is usually caused by an infection from food or water and settles within 2–3 days. The main danger is dehydration (losing too much water and salt).

## Self-care
- Drink oral rehydration solution (ORS). Prepare it exactly as written on the packet with clean drinking water, and make a fresh batch every 24 hours.
- Sip small amounts often, especially after every loose stool.
- Keep eating light food: rice, curd rice, bananas, khichdi, toast, idli.
- Avoid fruit juices, fizzy drinks, very sweet drinks, oily food and alcohol.
- Wash hands with soap after using the toilet and before eating or cooking.
- Check for dehydration: passing less urine, dark urine, dry mouth, dizziness.

## See a doctor if
- Diarrhoea lasts more than 2 days in adults, or more than 1 day in children.
- There is a high fever, severe stomach cramps, or you cannot keep fluids down.
- You are elderly, pregnant, diabetic or have kidney disease.
- You have recently travelled or several people who ate the same food are unwell.

## Emergency signs - call 112 / 108
- Blood in the stools or black stools.
- Signs of severe dehydration: no urine for 8 hours, very dry mouth, sunken eyes, confusion, fainting.
- A child who is very drowsy, floppy or not drinking.
- Severe, constant stomach pain.
