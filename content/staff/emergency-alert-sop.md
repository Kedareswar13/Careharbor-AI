# Emergency alert response (staff SOP)

## When an emergency alert appears
CareHarbor AI raises an **urgent emergency ticket** when a patient's message contains a red flag (for example chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs, suicidal thoughts). The patient has already been told to call 112 / 108.

## Steps for the front desk
- Open the ticket within **2 minutes** and inform the duty doctor.
- Call the patient back on the registered number. If there is no answer, try twice more 3 minutes apart.
- If the patient is on site, bring the crash cart and move them to the observation bay.
- If the patient needs transport, call 108 and stay on the line with the patient.
- For suicidal thoughts, stay with the patient and share Tele-MANAS 14416.

## Closing the ticket
- Record the outcome (call back reached / ambulance arranged / not reachable) in the ticket.
- Mark the ticket **closed** only after the duty doctor has reviewed it.

Internal document - not for patients.
