# Pharmacist callback (staff SOP)

## When this applies
CareHarbor AI never changes doses or suggests medicines. Questions such as "can I stop", "can I double", "missed dose" or "side effects" create a **pharmacist ticket**.

## Steps for the pharmacist
- Call back within **30 minutes** during pharmacy hours (8 AM - 8 PM).
- Verify the patient's identity again on the phone (name + date of birth).
- Check the prescription record and allergies before advising.
- For a suspected severe reaction (swelling, breathing trouble), advise 112 / 108 immediately and inform the duty doctor.
- Document the advice given in the ticket and close it.

Internal document - not for patients.
